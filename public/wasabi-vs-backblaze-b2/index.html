Objective Retention, Ratio & Engineering Caveats

  • Backblaze B2 3x Crossover Point: At 20 TB stored + 50 TB/mo egress (2.5x ratio), B2 bills $0 bandwidth ($120/mo total). At 10 TB stored + 50 TB/mo egress (5x ratio), 20 TB exceeds the 30 TB free cap, adding $200/mo ($260/mo total) unless fronted by a Bandwidth Alliance CDN.
  • Wasabi 90-Day Retention & 1:1 Fair-Use Policy: Wasabi charges $0.00699/GB ($0 egress & $0 API calls), but objects deleted before 90 days incur timed-deleted storage charges, and sustained monthly egress exceeding active stored volume (>1:1 ratio) triggers fair-use review.
  • High-Churn Escape Valves (Storj & R2): For CI/CD artifacts or ephemeral AI datasets overwritten daily, Storj ($0.004/GB + $0.007/GB egress, no retention minimum) and Cloudflare R2 ($0.015/GB + $0 egress) avoid timed-deletion billing.

FinOps Engineering FAQ

Is Wasabi or Backblaze B2 cheaper for 20 TB stored and 50 TB/mo outbound transfer?

At 20 TB stored + 50 TB/mo egress (2.5x ratio), Backblaze B2 costs $120/mo ($1,440/yr) because 50 TB sits inside B2's 60 TB (3x) free egress allowance, while Wasabi costs $139.80/mo ($1,678/yr) and flags its 1:1 egress-to-storage fair-use policy.

What happens to Backblaze B2 vs Wasabi pricing at 10 TB stored and 50 TB/mo egress?

At 10 TB stored + 50 TB/mo egress (5x ratio), Backblaze B2's 3x free cap covers 30 TB and bills $0.01/GB on the remaining 20 TB ($260/mo total for direct downloads, or $60/mo via Cloudflare CDN). Cloudflare R2 bills $150/mo unconditionally with zero ratio limits.

How does Wasabi's 90-day minimum storage retention impact monthly cloud bills?

If a 10 TB dataset is uploaded and deleted every 15 days on Wasabi, you simultaneously pay for 1 active copy plus up to 5 timed-deleted copies across a 90-day rolling window (~6x effective storage cost). Backblaze B2, Cloudflare R2, and Storj have 0-day minimum retention on standard Pay-As-You-Go tiers.
