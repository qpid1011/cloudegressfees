FinOps Engineering Notes — Cloud Egress Fees & Policy Caveats

1. Why do AWS S3 ($0.09/GB), GCP ($0.12/GB), and Azure ($0.087/GB) bills spike on read-heavy workloads?

At 20 TB stored and 50 TB/mo outbound internet egress, storage on AWS S3 costs just $460/mo while outbound bandwidth adds $4,491/mo ($4,951/mo total after 100 GB free, or $4,960/mo unadjusted — nearly $60,000/yr). Switching read-heavy public assets, AI checkpoints, or media origins to zero-egress S3-compatible storage cuts annual spend by $55,000–$57,972/yr.

2. What are the hidden engineering caveats behind "$0.00/GB Zero Egress" clouds?

90-Day Minimum Retention (Wasabi / Impossible Cloud): Objects deleted or overwritten before 30–90 days are still billed for the remaining retention window—ideal for backups/media, costly for high-churn temp files. 3× Stored Multiplier (Backblaze B2): Direct egress is free up to 3× your average monthly stored volume ($0.01/GB beyond), or unlimited $0 when fronted by Cloudflare, Fastly, or Bunny CDN. Class A/B Operations (Cloudflare R2): R2 charges $0 egress at any ratio, but high-frequency small-file PUT/LIST ($4.50/million) and GET ($0.36/million) calls should be batched or cached.

3. How is live telemetry verified?

Every 6 hours via GitHub Actions, sync_egress_fees.py queries the public AWS Price List API (AmazonS3/current/region_index.json) for offer publication metadata and probes official pricing/API endpoints across all 18 providers, archiving daily snapshots under /history/YYYY-MM-DD.json.
