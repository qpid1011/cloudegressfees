Objective Retention, Ratio & Engineering Caveats

  • EU Jurisdiction & CLOUD Act Isolation: Impossible Cloud (Germany), Hetzner (Germany/Finland), OVHcloud (France), Scaleway (France), Bunny.net (Slovenia), and Gcore (Luxembourg) operate EU-incorporated infrastructure for strict GDPR / NIS2 data sovereignty.
  • Zero vs Ultra-Low EU Egress: Impossible Cloud charges $0.00/GB egress ($0.0079/GB storage, $1,896/yr at 20 TB + 50 TB/mo), while Hetzner Object Storage charges $0.0053/GB storage + ~$0.0011/GB over 1 TB ($1,919/yr)—both 97% below AWS S3 ($59,412/yr).
  • Multi-AZ & Internal vRack Transfer: Scaleway Multi-AZ (Paris/Amsterdam/Warsaw, $9,230/yr) and OVHcloud ($8,352/yr) provide $0.00/GB private network/vRack transfer to European GPU and Kubernetes nodes.

FinOps Engineering FAQ

Which EU sovereign S3 storage providers have the lowest egress fees?

Impossible Cloud offers $0.00/GB zero egress on GDPR/ISO-certified European S3 storage ($158/mo or $1,896/yr for 20 TB stored + 50 TB/mo egress), followed closely by Hetzner Object Storage at ~$0.0011/GB after 1 TB included ($1,919/yr).

How do OVHcloud, Scaleway, Bunny.net, and Gcore compare on European bandwidth pricing?

At 20 TB stored + 50 TB/mo public egress, Bunny.net costs $8,400/yr ($0.01/GB NA/EU CDN), OVHcloud costs $8,352/yr ($0.011/GB public, $0 vRack), Gcore costs $8,592/yr (~$0.01/GB), and Scaleway Multi-AZ costs $9,230/yr—each saving over $50,000/yr vs AWS S3 ($59,412/yr).

Can US zero-egress providers pin buckets to EU regions, and how does that differ from EU sovereignty?

Cloudflare R2, Wasabi, and Backblaze B2 support EU bucket jurisdiction/region pinning for latency and data locality, whereas EU-headquartered providers (Hetzner, OVHcloud, Scaleway, Impossible Cloud) additionally avoid US CLOUD Act parent-entity jurisdiction.
